# Changelog

## 1.0.0 – 2026-11-21

First release.

### Convert images
- Open PNG, JPEG, WebP, TIFF, BMP, GIF, AVIF, ICO and SVG.
- Save as PNG, WebP (lossless or lossy), JPEG, TIFF (LZW or uncompressed), BMP and GIF.
- Exact pixel reading and built-in encoders for truly lossless conversion.
- Pixel-by-pixel verification of every lossless result.
- Color profiles kept for PNG, WebP, TIFF and JPEG.
- Batch conversion with ZIP download.

### Make icons
- Multi-size Windows `.ico` files from 16 to 256 px.
- Fit modes: fit, max, crop (with position) and scale.
- Trim transparent edges, padding, and per-file padding.
- Explorer preview in every view, in dark and light mode.
